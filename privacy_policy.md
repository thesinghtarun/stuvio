# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Privacy First

STUVIO is designed with privacy in mind. Your academic data belongs to
you, and we never sell your personal information.

## Information We Collect

STUVIO stores only the information required to provide its features,
including your profile details, workspaces, subjects, PDFs, notes,
assignments, and reminders on your device.

## Files & Documents

Your study materials remain yours. Imported PDFs, notes, and other
academic resources are stored locally on your device unless you
explicitly enable cloud synchronization in a future version.

## Cloud Sync

When cloud backup becomes available, only authenticated users will be
able to access their own files. We do not access, modify, or share your
documents.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to help protect your
information. While no system is completely secure, we continuously
improve our security practices.

## Permissions

STUVIO may request access to storage for importing PDFs, notifications
for assignment reminders, and camera access for document scanning. These
permissions are only used for their intended functionality.

## Your Rights

You can edit or remove your information at any time. Future updates will
also include options for exporting your data and permanently deleting
your account.

## Policy Updates

Our Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect improvements
or legal requirements. Significant changes will be communicated within
the application.
